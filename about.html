Hi! My name is Meighan Lindstrom, and I am a 23-year-old artist currently based outside of Detroit.

I create playful, whimsical, uplifting art, usually centered around themes of hope, compassion, and encouragement.

At the heart of my practice is a curiosity about what it means to be human. I love connecting with people through shared experiences of love, disruption, uncertainty, humanity, and, most of all, hope.

I love connecting and collaborating with new people! If you want to connect, please send me an email:
