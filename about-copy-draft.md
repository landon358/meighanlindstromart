## Lede

I draw small pictures and write on them in my own *handwriting.* That is most
of it, honestly.

## Body

Everything starts on paper — pencil, marker, crayon, a bit of paint, and
fairly often a colour that goes well past the line it was meant to stay
inside. I leave those bits in. A wobbly line is more honest than a straight
one, and I have never once been moved by a perfect circle.

The subjects repeat because the days do. Flowers in a glass vase. A martini
somebody clearly needed. A crowd of coats on a Midtown corner, all of them
somebody. Stars, constantly. And words — the sentence you wish a friend had
said to you at the right moment, written down and handed over a little late
but still.

I make things to be given away. A print for a kitchen wall, a drawing for
someone having a hard year, lettering for a wedding that people look at long
after the flowers are gone. If it ends up taped inside a cupboard door, that
is the highest compliment available.

Prints and originals are available, and commissions are open. If something
here belongs on your wall, or you have an idea that needs drawing, say hello.
